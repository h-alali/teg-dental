Endodontic Treatments

Clear explanations of common specialist endodontic treatments and why they may be recommended.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment addresses inflamed or infected tissue inside the tooth. The goal is to remove diseased tissue, disinfect the internal canal system and preserve the natural tooth when clinically appropriate.

When it may be considered

  • Persistent tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Deep decay or pulpal inflammation
  • Infection related to the tooth pulp
  • Selected traumatic injuries

Root Canal Retreatment

A previously treated tooth can sometimes remain symptomatic or develop new disease. Retreatment involves reassessing the tooth and, when appropriate, cleaning and treating the canal system again.

Apicoectomy

An apicoectomy is endodontic microsurgery involving the root end and surrounding tissue. It may be considered in selected situations when conventional treatment alone cannot resolve disease.

Dental Trauma and Cracked Teeth

Specialist assessment can help determine the extent of injury, the condition of the pulp and whether the tooth can be predictably retained.
